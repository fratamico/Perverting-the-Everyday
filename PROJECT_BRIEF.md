# Fishbowl

Build an interactive website prototype within the existing Perverting the Everyday catalogue as a product page.

## Concept
Tagline: “Ignorance is bliss.” Fishbowl contact lenses filter the wearer’s visual reality. Optional premium earpieces filter audio. Transparent lenses have a faint aqua ring; tiny pearl-white earpieces share a white compact case.

Users subscribe to reality filters from developers, communities, institutions, and creators, enable multiple filters, arrange priority, and adjust immersion. Advertising is sincere, calm, and aspirational; unsettling implications emerge through ordinary features.

## Three connected experiences
1. Product landing page introducing lenses, optional audio upgrade, and subscriptions, anchored by the supplied physical product advertisement.
2. Filter marketplace and personal stack: browse details, simulate subscriptions, toggle filters, reorder priority, adjust immersion, and see the same scene through different filters. Include Everyday Grace (Christian publisher), Cottagecore (pastoral botanical transformations), and Good News Only (suppresses distressing information).
3. Fishbowl Studio: intent and publisher identity; visual, audio, and behavioral rules; conditions and transformations; original/filtered simulator; testing against subscribed filters; conflict inspection and priority resolution; versioning and simulated publishing. Example: Good News Only hides encounters Everyday Grace would use for reflection.

## Design and implementation
Follow supplied mockups: warm white, restrained black typography, subtle aqua, generous whitespace, premium photography. Keep hardware consistent. Interfaces must be functional components, not screenshots. Use mock data and persistent local state; simulate subscription and publishing. Pricing TBD. Retain existing stack where possible. Inspect supplied references, implement, and run locally. Use a couple of supplied images within the product page.
